# Video questions for the task 1 recording

One 2 to 3 minute recording unblocks task 1, which is 4 of the proposal's 10 metrics. The clip
feeds three things: transcription (word error rate), sentiment, and career directions. Answer
the questions below and the same transcript can also run through skill extraction, matching,
and the skill-gap feature, so one clip demos all four tasks at once.

Record as yourself. Say tool names out loud, because those are what the extractor pulls out
and matches against the postings.

## Background and skills

These give clear speech for transcription and real skills for the extractor to find.

1. Tell me about yourself and your role. What do you do day to day?
2. What tools and technologies do you work with most? Name them specifically, like Python,
   SQL, Excel, Tableau.
3. Walk me through a recent project you worked on and what you built.

## Sentiment

Give both a positive and a negative, or the sentiment model has nothing to separate.

4. What part of your work do you enjoy most, and why?
5. What is the most frustrating or difficult part of your job right now?
6. How do you feel about where your career is at today?

## Career directions

These feed the career-directions task and give the matching and gap steps a target.

7. Where do you want your career to go in the next 2 to 3 years? Name a specific role.
8. What skills do you think you need to learn to get there?
9. What kind of role or company would be your ideal next step?

## You don't need all 9

Questions 2, 4, 5, 7, and 8 alone cover all three sub-tasks and fit inside 2 to 3 minutes.

## Recording checklist

- Length 2 to 3 minutes. Shorter starves the metrics; longer is fine but not needed.
- One speaker, quiet room, phone or laptop mic is fine. Whisper handles ordinary audio well.
- Save as a common format: .mp3, .m4a, .wav, or a .mp4 with clear audio.
- Give one clear positive answer and one honest frustration, so sentiment has a real contrast.
- Name a concrete next role in question 7, like "data engineer" or "product manager," so the
  matching step has something to aim at.
- Name at least 5 or 6 real tools across your answers.
- Speak the tool names clearly. "C sharp" and "dot net" get transcribed better said plainly.
- Put the file where the pipeline can reach it, and note the path for whoever runs task 1.
